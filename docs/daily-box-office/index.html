Why day-by-day box office tracking matters

Unlike cumulative lifetime grosses that reward long runs, day-wise tracking captures theatrical momentum as it happens. Tracking Day 1 opening strength, Friday-to-Saturday jump percentages, Monday holds, and second-weekend drops reveals the actual theatrical health and word-of-mouth of every release.

Indian Box Office publishes weighted-median consensus numbers across trade trackers for every reported day. Click any film above to view its complete Day 1 to closing run breakdown.
