About Indian Box Office

An independent consensus desk for Indian theatrical collections — built to answer “what did it do today?” with Day 1 through closing-run clarity.

What we publish

Indian Box Office (indian-boxoffice.com) publishes a live theatrical board, day-wise film pages, language hubs, 2026 rankings, Day 1 records, and a daily morning brief. The product focus is deliberate: day-by-day India nett and worldwide gross, not only lifetime leaderboards that flatten the first two weeks of a release.

Coverage spans Hindi / Bollywood, Tamil / Kollywood, Telugu / Tollywood, Malayalam / Mollywood, Kannada / Sandalwood, and major Hollywood titles in Indian theatres. New releases are picked up as soon as usable daily reports exist.

What we are not

We are not an official auditor, not a studio PR desk, and not a scrapbook of unverified social-media screenshots. When the primary day-wise spine fails, we repair the ingest rather than invent rows. When AdSense or other advertising is enabled, ads are secondary to the editorial board — see the privacy and editorial pages for how that is disclosed.

How to use the site

Start with the home board for this week’s daily chart, open a film for the full day-wise table and analysis, read the morning brief for context, and browse the brief archive for prior mornings. Language hubs isolate industry runs when you only care about one circuit.

Independent consensus of published theatrical estimates. India has no official auditor — we publish the spread. Figures in ₹ crore.
