    Independent desk

    Why Indian Box Office exists

    India has no official theatrical auditor. This site publishes a weighted-median consensus of day-by-day India nett and worldwide gross so readers can follow Day 1 openings, weekday holds, and closing runs without treating any single headline as gospel. Developed by Mr Kevin Boyjonauth.

    Read the about page, methodology, editorial policy, and morning brief — or contact the desk for corrections.

    Questions

    Box office questions, answered

    How often is the Indian box office collection updated?

    The board refreshes about every three hours through the day, and a written morning brief is published each morning. Day-wise collection figures update as soon as new numbers clear the consensus pipeline.

    What is the difference between India nett and worldwide gross?

    India nett is the domestic collection after entertainment tax and GST are removed. Worldwide gross is the total global ticket revenue before tax deductions, including overseas markets. The two are never interchangeable, which is why this desk reports them separately.

    Which languages and film industries are covered?

    Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada and Punjabi theatrical releases are covered, along with major Hollywood titles in Indian cinemas. New releases are picked up as soon as usable daily reports exist.

    Are these box office figures official?

    No. India has no official box office auditor. Every published figure is an estimate. This desk computes a weighted-median consensus across multiple independent trade reports rather than relying on a single reported number. See the methodology page.

    How do I request a correction?

    Email desk@indian-boxoffice.com with the film title, date, and the figure you believe is wrong. Material corrections follow the editorial policy.
