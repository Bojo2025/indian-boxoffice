    Questions

    Box office questions, answered

    How often is the Indian box office collection updated?

    The board refreshes every three hours through the day, and a written morning brief is published each morning at 08:00. Day-wise collection figures update as soon as new numbers are reported.

    What is the difference between India nett and worldwide gross?

    India nett is the domestic collection after entertainment tax and GST are removed. Worldwide gross is the total global ticket revenue before tax deductions, including overseas markets. The two are never interchangeable, which is why this desk reports them separately.

    Which languages and film industries are covered?

    All Indian theatrical releases are covered — Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada and Punjabi films, along with Hollywood titles released in India. New releases are picked up automatically as soon as they begin reporting collections.

    Are these box office figures official?

    India has no official box office auditor. Every published figure is an estimate, so this desk computes a weighted-median consensus across multiple trade trackers rather than relying on a single reported number.
