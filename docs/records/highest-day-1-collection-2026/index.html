Understanding Day 1 (opening day) collections

Opening day collection reflects pre-release hype, star power, screen count, and advance ticket bookings. On this desk, Day 1 figures represent verified consensus India nett (post-tax domestic revenue) reported on the film's first official day in cinemas.
