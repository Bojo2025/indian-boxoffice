About our box office methodology

How Indian Box Office builds an independent, day-by-day consensus of theatrical collections across Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam and Kannada releases — and why we refuse to treat any single headline as fact.

Why a consensus desk exists

India has no official box office auditor. Trade outlets publish estimates that can diverge on the same film, the same day, by crores. Fans and journalists still need a clear picture of Day 1 openings, weekday holds, and closing runs. Indian Box Office exists to turn those competing estimates into a transparent, repeatedly updated consensus — with the day-wise spine kept in public view.

We are not a studio marketing channel. We do not invent openings to flatter a release. When sources disagree, the desk shows the consensus total and keeps refreshing as later reports arrive.

Weighted-median consensus

Every published figure on this site is an estimate. The desk therefore computes a weighted-median consensus across multiple independent trade reports rather than reprinting any single reported number as gospel.

Each contributing report is weighted by historical reliability and coverage for that money field (India nett, India gross, overseas, worldwide). A weighted median dampens outliers without throwing away minority reports that may later prove correct. When the spine that supplies day-wise rows is temporarily unavailable, the board falls back carefully and surfaces desk health alerts for editors — visitor pages never blame named trackers in the brief copy.

What “day-by-day” means here

Lifetime worldwide charts alone hide the story of a theatrical run. This desk prioritises:

  • Day 1 (opening day) India nett as soon as a usable report exists.
  • Daily India nett through the run, with percentage change versus the previous reported day when available.
  • Weekend and Week 1 milestones derived from the same day-wise ledger.
  • Worldwide gross kept separate from India nett so readers are not forced to confuse tax bases.

Film pages therefore carry both a day-wise table and a short editorial analysis written from that film’s own numbers — not a duplicated marketing synopsis.

Update cadence

The board refreshes multiple times through the Indian business day (scheduled ingest about every three hours). A written morning brief is published each morning with chart context, openers, and hold notes. Day-wise collection figures update as soon as new numbers clear the consensus pipeline.

Definitions

  • India nett — domestic theatrical collection after entertainment tax and GST are removed.
  • India gross — domestic theatrical ticket revenue before those tax deductions.
  • Worldwide gross — total global ticket revenue before tax deductions, including overseas markets.
  • Occupancy / shows — when reported, used as context for a day’s strength; they are not substitutes for nett.

These figures are never interchangeable. The desk always labels India nett and worldwide gross separately.

Verdicts and budgets

Hit / average / flop style labels on this site are working desk labels based on available budget estimates and the shape of the run. If budget is missing, verdicts stay provisional. Corrections follow the editorial policy.
